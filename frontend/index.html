PolyMerge

PolyMerge helps researchers inspect multi-disease treatment candidates across knowledge-graph evidence, deterministic safety rules, optimization output, and traditional ML interaction severity predictions.

Problem

Multi-disease treatment research involves many candidate drugs, interactions, and evidence sources that are difficult to inspect together.

Purpose

PolyMerge combines knowledge graph evidence, traditional ML DDI severity prediction, deterministic safety rules, candidate generation, optimization, and explainability.

Target User

Researchers, pharmacists, and academic reviewers who need traceable research decision support.

Research Disclaimer

PolyMerge does not provide prescriptions, dosage recommendations, clinical safety guarantees, or clinical efficacy guarantees.
